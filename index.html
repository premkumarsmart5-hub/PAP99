My trades (UAE time)

How it works: for each pair the app downloads about 5000 candles and reads 37 values (RSI, MACD, Stochastic, ADX, CCI, Aroon, Bollinger, EMAs, 15 candle patterns, support and resistance, time of day). A tree-boosting AI trained in the background predicts the next move, and 8 classic strategies are tested alongside it. Only what beats break-even on unseen history is VALIDATED (quality A+ = AI and a strategy agree, A = AI, B = strategy). A dashed yellow setup is the AI's best guess but is not proven: use the demo account for it. Analysis of a new candle takes a few milliseconds; the wait is the free data feed. Nothing can guarantee wins.
