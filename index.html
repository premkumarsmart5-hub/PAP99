My trades (UAE time)

How it works: for each pair the app downloads about 5000 five-minute candles and reads 49 values (RSI, MACD, Stochastic, ADX, CCI, Aroon, Bollinger squeeze, EMAs, RSI divergence, 32 candle patterns scored by location plus patterns on the 15-minute candle, support and resistance, 15-minute and 1-hour trend, time of day). Two AI models trained in the background (gradient-boosted trees + logistic regression) must agree on the direction for the chosen expiry (5, 10 or 15 min), and 10 classic strategies plus every candle pattern are tested on this pair's own history. Only what beats break-even on unseen history is VALIDATED (quality A+ = AI and a strategy agree, A = AI, B = strategy). A dashed yellow setup is the AI's best guess but is not proven: use the demo account for it. Analysis of a new candle takes a few milliseconds; the wait is the free data feed. Nothing can guarantee wins.
